Ultra-Fast Media Stream

Combines 320kbps MP3/FLAC and 4K downloads with zero-latency tab music recognition on the desktop.

Overview

StreamPulse is a modern, open-source Electron desktop application and Chrome Manifest V3 extension designed to make downloading high-fidelity media from YouTube, YouTube Music, and web sources effortless and lightning-fast.

StreamPulse Desktop Main Interface — YouTube & Music Grabber

Problem

Traditional web-based downloader services are filled with intrusive ads, slow conversion queues, and usually compress audio down to subpar 128kbps quality. Furthermore, there is no seamless bridge to instantly identify music playing in browser tabs and queue it directly for high-resolution desktop downloads.

Solution

StreamPulse establishes an instant local bridge between the browser and desktop engine. With an integrated AI acoustic fingerprinting extension, it identifies background songs across TikTok, Instagram Reels, Twitter/X, and browser tabs, dispatching downloads with full ID3 metadata and album covers at up to 320kbps MP3 or lossless FLAC.

Live Download Queue — Multithreaded Processing Engine

Key Features

  • High-Fidelity Audio: 320kbps studio MP3, lossless FLAC, and M4A audio extraction.
  • 4K 60FPS Video: 2160p (4K), 1440p (2K), and 1080p crystal-clear MP4 downloads.
  • Batch Playlist Engine: Download entire playlists with organized folder structures and embedded artwork.
  • Chrome Manifest V3 Extension: Real-time fluid visualizer, zero-latency tab music recognition, and desktop bridge.
  • USB & Car Sync: One-click transfer to USB drives and in-car audio systems.
  • Bilingual & Themed: Full Turkish and English localization with Dynamic Dark and Light modes.

Library & History — Media Management, Playlists & Flash Drive Sync

Technical Approach

Engineered with Electron, React 19, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. Bundled native FFmpeg and yt-dlp binaries ensure hardware-accelerated processing. The companion extension is built strictly following Chrome Manifest V3 standards.

Settings & Engine Config — Concurrency, Download Directories & Formats

My Role

Product architecture, Electron desktop app, Chrome MV3 extension, audio processing pipelines, local communication protocol, and UI/UX design.
